/
The part nobody else does

No API keys. No second bill. Slashbar borrows the AI you already pay for.

01

Your subscription, your tokens

Slashbar ships no AI of its own. It drives the CLIs you've already logged into — Claude, Codex, Gemini — Apple Intelligence on Macs that have it, or a fully local Ollama. We never see a prompt.

02

Local-first, by default

Native SwiftUI. Real window vibrancy, and a Carbon global hotkey that needs no Accessibility permission. Tokens live in the Keychain. No Slashbar server, ever — nothing you type is uploaded.

03

The field is the input

Type or paste anything — content-aware ranking puts the right action first. Press Tab to search every action by name.

Set it up — in about a minute

Already pay for Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini? Install its CLI — claude, codex or gemini — and sign in once in Terminal. Slashbar detects it automatically; switch providers any time from the chip in the palette footer, or in Settings → AI provider.

On an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26+? Apple Intelligence answers on-device — nothing to install, nothing leaves your Mac. (This is what "on-device" means here; it isn't available on Intel or older macOS.)

Prefer fully local models? Run Ollama — Slashbar auto-detects it at localhost:11434 and lists every model you've pulled, zero configuration. 100% offline, and it works on Intel Macs too.

None of the above? Pick Manual browser: Slashbar builds the prompt, copies it, and opens the AI chat of your choice — paste and go. Translate actions even fall back to Google Translate on their own, so they work with zero setup.
