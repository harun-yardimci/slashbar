Privacy / Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026

The short version: Slashbar runs locally on your Mac and has no account system. The app sends anonymous launch counts to TelemetryDeck; this website measures traffic with Google Analytics. We never send the text you type or copy, your files, integration data, or other content.

Who we are

Slashbar is a macOS application made by Harun Yardımcı (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at hello@slashbar.app.

What we collect

From the app, we collect a small amount of anonymous usage data through TelemetryDeck: app launches, app version, operating system version, and device class. TelemetryDeck processes this data without advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. From the website, we collect traffic data through Google Analytics — see This website below. We do not send names, email addresses, the text you type or copy, files, clipboard contents, AI prompts, integration data, or persistent hardware identifiers.

What stays on your device

Everything Slashbar remembers lives locally on your Mac and never leaves it through us:

  • The text you type or paste, and the actions you run on it.
  • Your settings, preferences, favorites, persona/memory, and recent clipboard history.
  • Access tokens for any services you choose to connect — stored in the macOS Keychain, not in plain files.

AI features use the provider you choose

Slashbar's AI actions run through tools you already have: the command-line tools you've logged into (such as Claude, Codex/OpenAI, or Gemini), or Apple's on-device Foundation Models. When you run an AI action, the text you act on is sent directly from your Mac to the provider you selected, governed by that provider's own privacy policy and terms. Slashbar does not proxy, see, or store these requests. On-device models don't leave your Mac at all.

Connected services (integrations)

If you choose to connect a third-party service — for example GitHub, Spotify, Google, Microsoft/Teams, Telegram, Linear, Jira, Notion, Slack, or GitLab — Slashbar talks to that service directly from your Mac, using credentials you provide that are stored in your Keychain. That data flows only between your device and that service and is governed by its terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store it.

Network connections

Slashbar sends anonymous app-launch analytics to TelemetryDeck and makes one housekeeping request to GitHub for the latest released version number. All other network activity is to the AI providers or services you explicitly use.

This website

slashbar.app is a static site hosted on Cloudflare. It measures traffic two ways. TelemetryDeck counts anonymous page views without cookies; its processing is described in its privacy policy. Google Analytics 4 measures visits in more detail: it sets first-party cookies in your browser (named _ga and _ga_*) to recognise returning visits, and records your approximate location derived from your IP address, your device and browser, how you arrived at the site, and the pages, scrolls and outbound links you interact with. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising or ad personalisation. Google's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy and in how Google uses data from sites that use its services. As the network host, Cloudflare may process basic request information (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the site, under Cloudflare's privacy policy. App downloads are served from GitHub Releases, subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Slashbar is a general-purpose developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version here.
